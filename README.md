## What is MuseArc?

MuseArc teaches you to read sheet music and recognize musical notes by ear. You work through 60 levels. The lessons start with simple treble clef notes and advance to jazz harmony. The app works completely offline. It is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license.

## Who Should Use This App

- Beginners who want to learn music theory
- Musicians who want to improve their ear training
- Students who need to practice sight-reading
- Anyone who wants to develop perfect pitch recognition
- Teachers looking for a free tool for their students

## 🎯 Key Features

**60 Progressive Levels**
Each level builds on the last. You start with individual notes on the treble clef. Later levels introduce bass clef, intervals, chords, and jazz harmony.

**Ear Training**
The app plays a note. You identify it by sound alone. This builds your ability to recognize pitch without a reference.

**Sight Reading Practice**
See a note on the staff. Click the correct note name. This trains your brain to read music faster.

**Offline Operation**
No internet connection is needed after download. Use MuseArc anywhere - on a plane, in a park, or in a basement studio.

**Free and Open Source**
No ads. No in-app purchases. No data collection. The code is public for anyone to inspect or improve.

## 🎮 How to Use MuseArc

The interface is simple. You see a musical staff with notes. You hear a tone. You click the correct answer.

**For Sight Reading:**
1. Look at the note on the staff
2. Click the button with the correct note name (C, D, E, F, G, A, B)
3. The app tells you if you are correct
4. Move to the next note

**For Ear Training:**
1. Click the "Play" button to hear a note
2. Listen carefully
3. Click the note name you heard
4. The app shows the correct answer

**Progress Tracking:**
Each level shows your score. You need 80% correct to unlock the next level. You can replay any level to improve your score.

## 📚 Level Overview

The 60 levels split into six groups:

**Levels 1-10:** Treble clef notes. Learn to read and recognize the notes on the lines and spaces.

**Levels 11-20:** Bass clef notes. Extend your reading to lower notes.

**Levels 21-30:** Intervals. Learn to hear the distance between two notes (major third, perfect fifth, etc.).

**Levels 31-40:** Chords. Identify major, minor, diminished, and augmented chords.

**Levels 41-50:** Key signatures. Recognize the key of a piece by the sharps or flats.

**Levels 51-60:** Jazz harmony. Work with seventh chords, extended chords, and complex progressions.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**The app will not start.**
Make sure you have Windows 10 or newer. Restart your computer. Try running the installer again.

**I hear no sound.**
Check your computer volume. Make sure your speakers are on. Check that MuseArc is not muted in the Windows volume mixer (right-click the speaker icon in the taskbar).

**The installer says "Windows protected your PC".**
Click "More info" then "Run anyway". This warning appears because the app is not from the Microsoft Store. The software is safe.

**The app looks too small on my screen.**
Go to the app settings. Increase the "Zoom" or "Scale" option. You can also change your Windows display scaling.
